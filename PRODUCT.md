# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Business owners, procurement officers, and warehouse/logistics managers in the Philippines researching forklift equipment — comparing suppliers, requesting quotes, or evaluating after-sales service before committing to a purchase.

## Product Purpose

A Philippine sales agent website for HELI forklifts. It exists to present the full HELI equipment lineup, establish credibility as a local authorized sales agent, and convert visitors into leads via quote requests and direct contact.

## Positioning

Local HELI sales agent in the Philippines — the direct point of contact for purchasing genuine HELI forklifts with local after-sales support, in a market where buyers prefer working with an in-country representative rather than importing directly.

## Operating Context

Buyers browse on desktop and mobile. They compare multiple suppliers. They want to see real equipment photos, understand product specs, and have a clear path to get a price. Trust signals (authorized agent status, event photos, service offerings) are critical conversion factors.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Products: lithium battery forklifts, electric forklifts, diesel forklifts, reach trucks, electric storage equipment, heavy forklifts, warehouse trucks, wheel loaders
- Services: maintenance, repair, installation, operator training, consulting, customization
- Events: Bocaue client display, Malaysia international exhibit
- Serves Metro Manila and surrounding regions across the Philippines
- Dual deployment: Vercel (primary) + GitHub Pages
- Contact: info@heliforklift.ph

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Heli Forklift Philippines
- Visual reference: helichina.net — blue/corporate palette, professional modular layout
- HELI brand identity must be respected (not invented claims beyond agent scope)

## Evidence on Hand

- Real product photos organized by category in /public/Heli/
- Event photos: /public/Events/ (Bocaue display + Malaysia exhibit)
- OG image: /public/og-image.jpg

## Product Principles

1. Credibility first — buyers need to trust a local agent before they'll commit; every page must earn that trust
2. Equipment leads — the product lineup is the reason visitors come; make it easy to browse and compare
3. Local presence — Philippines context (location, language cadence, service reach) must feel native, not translated
4. Clear path to contact — every section should have a frictionless route to a quote request
5. Brand fidelity — visual identity follows HELI China's corporate standard; no improvised color or style

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard web accessibility (keyboard navigation, sufficient contrast). No specific requirements stated.
